# PROBLEM STATEMENT
**The Problem:**
Music is a huge part of entertainment that we enjoy every single day. However listening to the same songs on loop can get tiring. Students especially struggle a lot to find new recommendations through their busy day-to-day schedules. Existing solutions are very overwhelming as there are thousands of songs to browse through or require external accounts like Spotify, YouTube Music, Apple Music etc. Those services focus on global recommendations with millions of users making it hard for students to actually find out what their peers are listening to.

**Scope of the Project:**
- Music discovery takes time and is inefficient
- Collaborative recommendations (what friends like) are more trustworthy than algorithms
- Campus scale recommendations are more relevant than global scale ones
- Students want personalized suggestions without corporate music apps

**Our Solution to this:**
Music Match creates a collaborative music recommendation system where:
- Users can rate songs and build preference profiles
- The system learns from collective user ratings
- Recommendations are based on both personal taste and what similar users enjoy
- Students can discover music their peers actually listen to and connect with them

**Target Users:**
- Students
- Music enthusiasts in campus
- Anyone seeking collaborative music discovery

**High level Features:**
- User signup/login with authentication
- Add and rate songs and artists on a scale of 1-5
- Get personalized music recommendations based on user ratings
- View top rated and trending songs on campus
- See recommendation insights and match with similar users
- Interactive CLI interface with easy navigation
